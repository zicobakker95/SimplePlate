# Italian (it) localisation notes

## Style
Informal "tu" throughout (Imposta, Scegli, Inserisci). Friendly, short, no jargon. Sentence case for labels.

## For a native speaker to check
- "Apple Salute" (iOS app is just "Salute"; kept for clarity next to Health Connect).
- bmiObese "Obeso" (adjective; "Obesità" may be softer).
- waterGoalToast "Salute!" (toast pun; could be confused with the Health app).
- featAdFreeSub "interstitial o con premio" wording.
- Remaining "cibo" in prose (e.g. waterTracking none; "Cosa c'è nel tuo piatto?" fine).
- Greeting "Benvenuto" is masculine default.
