# PlateSimple glossary and style guide: Traditional Chinese (zh-Hant, Taiwan wording)

Locale code: `zh_Hant` (Play: `zh-TW`, App Store: `zh-Hant`). Taiwan wording is the base; no Simplified characters; all CJK characters must exist in Big5.

## Tone
Friendly, concise, second person "你" (not "您"). Full-width punctuation in Chinese sentences (，。！？：；（）「」). ASCII digits. Units keep the source spelling with a space: `100 g`, `100 ml`, `kcal`, `%`.
