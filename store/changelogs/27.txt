• Amounts can now show servings next to grams, like "100 g (2 servings)", whenever the food tells us its serving size.
• Custom foods can set their own serving size, so they show servings too.
• Tap any logged entry to edit it — including entries from previous days. Editing was already possible, but only by long-pressing, which was easy to miss.
